## Latest economic correction

The low-grade resource experiment initially made carbonaceous both less productive and more expensive than silicate. It now has a lower institutional price per physical crate and a 0.65 effective yield. Procurement compares delivered cost per effective unit, so it can be attractive locally or under supplier pressure without automatically beating compact ore over long routes.

Hauler growth existed before this session, but it was constrained by a region-wide six-ship cap and triggered by all ships being busy regardless of backlog. It now requires loadable waiting freight, permits up to four operational ships per carrier and twelve region-wide, and visibly reports when the carrier cannot afford the 6,000-credit investment. Emergency replacement finance remains distinct from ordinary expansion.

## What to watch in the next long run

- Whether cheap, bulky carbonaceous actually wins only under sensible distance/capacity conditions.
- Whether supplier reservations clear and freight waiting time falls rather than merely creating more work.
- Frequency and causes of `carrier.hireDeferred`; distinguish insufficient capitalization from unprofitable freight.
- Whether twelve regional haulers is sufficient and stable rather than an equilibrium target to fill.
- Whether populations resume final consumption and weaker hubs remain solvent.
- Whether repair queues receive steady work without immobilizing an entire industrial fleet.
- Whether protection losses are materially stressful without collapsing extraction and freight.
- Any growth in retained records, physical pickups or streamed entities that brings back periodic frame stalls.

## Strategic direction retained

The larger horizon remains farming, manufacturing, used assets and parts, hostile salvage, crime and piracy, merchants, finance, reliability and reputation, followed by higher authorities that issue and enforce mining, transit, trade, salvage, patrol and settlement rights. Those should grow out of the current institutional records and public work, not become isolated game modes.
